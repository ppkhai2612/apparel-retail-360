# Data Contract: Apparel Retail 360 Pipeline

This is what your finished Lakeflow pipeline must produce: every table and view, the schema it lands in, its type, its key and the columns it outputs. Names and paths come from `src/variables.py`; everything lands in the `apparel_store` catalog. The four source streams (sales, customers, products, stores) and their raw schemas are documented in [SyntheticDataGenerator.md](SyntheticDataGenerator.md), so they are not repeated here.
